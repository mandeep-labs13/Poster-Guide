How it works

Each poster runs three accounts — YouTube, Instagram and Facebook — through three stages: Warm-up (3 days) → Trial (5 days) → Clipper assignment or next step.

Every poster completes warm-up and trial. Views after the trial determine whether accounts move to a clipper or waiting list, or whether the poster recreates accounts or drops out.

Stage 1 · Warm-up

Build a niche-relevant feed before posting.

3 days · 30 min/day
Your targetWarm up each account for 30 minutes a day for 3 days. Do not post during warm-up. After day 3, you are paid a ₹100 setup fee.

Each session

Day 1

Finish profile; search the niche; watch videos to the end; follow 3–5 accounts; like 5–10 videos. Verify email and phone. No posting.

Day 2

Repeat the routine; follow 3–5 more accounts; leave 2–4 real comments; save a few videos. No posting.

Day 3

Repeat the routine. Check whether the feed is mostly niche. If not, spend extra time on the steps and tell your Post Head.

Do / Don't

Do: use the account only for its niche, vary sessions, verify email and phone on day 1, keep a daily note.

Don't: follow or like in bulk (stay under about 20 follows/day), repeat comments, use a second device, or delete and re-upload.

Stage 3 · After the trial

Use your trial result to confirm the next action.

Decision flow

Did your accounts get views?

Your Post Head confirms the result and the next step.

✓ Yes — accounts got views

  • You are assigned a clipper or wait until one is available.
  • In both cases, you earn the ₹1,000 monthly salary.
  • Once assigned, create your accounts on the website, download the clipper's videos and post them.

× No — accounts did not get views

  • Recreate your accounts; you receive a ₹100 recreation fee.
  • Or drop out.

Monthly salary₹1,000 from day 1 of posting to day 30, whether you are posting or waiting for a clipper. This is separate from the setup fee and trial bonus.

Flagged or shadowbanned accounts

Do not improvise. Follow the escalation steps.

Safety first
ImportantIf 2 or 3 accounts get flagged or shadowbanned, your choices are to recreate accounts or drop out. Recreating 1, 2 or 3 accounts has a ₹100 recreation fee.

Signs of a problem

  • “Action blocked” or “Try again later”.
  • Repeated checks or CAPTCHAs.
  • A removed or restricted video.
  • A copyright notice.
  • Non-follower views drop to almost nothing for three videos in a row.

What to do — in order

Before, during and after publishing

Title rules

  • Put the hook or subject first.
  • Copy the text shown at the top of the video as the title; do not rewrite it.
  • Add emojis that match the video's feeling.
  • Never use the same title twice on the same account.
  • Follow campaign-specific title instructions if different.

Exaggerate the feeling, not the facts. Do not promise what the video does not show.

Upload steps

Instagram: Create → Post → choose file → pick cover → add title and hashtags → Share.

YouTube: Studio → Create → Upload videos → choose file → add title and description → set audience → Public → Publish.

Facebook: open assigned Page → Reel → choose file → add title and hashtags → Publish.

Do not cross-post automaticallyPost on each platform separately. Do not use “also share to Facebook” or scheduling tools unless your Post Head says yes. After posting, do not keep editing the title or cover, and never delete and re-upload.

The six rules

Memorize these. They govern your daily workflow.

Must follow

01 · One page = one browser

Install a separate browser for each page (Brave, Firefox or Edge). Do not use your default browser.

02 · Never mix pages in one browser

Never log in to or out of different pages in the same browser, even in incognito.

03 · Use the same mobile data

Use your own mobile data from the same SIM every day. On a laptop, share your phone's hotspot. No hostel, public or shared Wi-Fi, VPN or free proxy.

04 · Work by hand

No bots, auto-posting tools, buying followers or views.

05 · One video per day

Post at the same time every day. If you miss a day, don't catch up by yourself; ask your Post Head.

06 · Stop and report problems

If something looks wrong, stop and tell your Post Head the same day. Do not try to fix it yourself.
